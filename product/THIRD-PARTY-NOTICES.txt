Verity local analysis integrations

Model weights and third-party runtimes are not bundled in the extension or
bootstrap payload. Setup separately downloads reviewed snapshots and packages.
Their model cards and installed LICENSE/NOTICE files must remain intact.
No upstream author endorses Verity or its accuracy. Model/data permissions are
separate; GPTSniffer source/checkpoint permissions and a production known-AI
code corpus remain unresolved and are not automatically supplied.

- Docling / docling-parse: MIT; native and transitive licenses in installed package.
- Microsoft Playwright: Apache-2.0; browser distribution has separate notices.
- Desklib ai-text-detector-v1.01: model card declares MIT; its mean-pooling and
  single-logit architecture inform the local adapter. Snapshot README is retained.
- BAAI bge-small-en-v1.5: model card declares MIT; snapshot README is retained.
- Sentence Transformers: Apache-2.0; installed LICENSE and NOTICE.txt retained.
- SameCode, AboutCode/nexB and contributors: Apache-2.0; installed NOTICE and

- Falcon-7B and Falcon-7B-Instruct, Technology Innovation Institute: model cards

  retained with installed Transformers and other Apache-licensed dependencies.
